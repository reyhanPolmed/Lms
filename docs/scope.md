# LMS Scope

This document strictly defines the functional scope of the Akara Learning Management System. 

**CRITICAL RULE:** Do not expand the LMS scope beyond the modules listed below. Do not add marketplace, payment gateways, social media feeds, complex gamification, video conferencing, AI tutors, ecommerce, blog systems, or HR systems unless explicitly required and documented here.

## Core Modules
1. **Authentication and Authorization**: Login, logout, password change, profile management.
2. **User Management**: Creating and managing user accounts across roles.
3. **Role Management**: Standard roles are limited to `student`, `teacher`, and `admin`.
4. **Student Profile**: Managing student data (NISN, class).
5. **Teacher Profile**: Managing teacher data (Employee ID/NIP, subjects).
6. **Classroom Management**: Organizing students into classrooms.
7. **Course/Subject Management**: Creating and structuring academic courses.
8. **Enrollment/Class Membership**: Assigning teachers to courses and students to classes.
9. **Learning Materials**: Managing text, PDF, video links, or file-based materials.
10. **Assignments**: Creating tasks with deadlines and maximum scores.
11. **Assignment Submissions**: Students uploading or linking their work, teachers reviewing.
12. **Quizzes/Exams**: Multiple choice quizzes with durations and schedules.
13. **Grades**: Recording and presenting scores from assignments and quizzes.
14. **Attendance**: Tracking student presence in classes or specific courses.
15. **Announcements**: Broadcast messages directed at specific roles.
16. **Basic Dashboard Summaries**: KPI metrics, progress summaries, and schedules.
17. **Notifications**: System alerts for deadlines, submissions, and feedback.
